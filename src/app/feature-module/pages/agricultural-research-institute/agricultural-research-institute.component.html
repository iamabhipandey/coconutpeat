Agricultural Research Institute

Innovating for sustainable agriculture through science

Our dedicated research institute focuses on improving the quality and efficacy of cocopeat. Through rigorous scientific testing and continuous development, we ensure optimal growth conditions for various crops.

Local Production Factory (Directly Owned & Operated)

Step 01

Coconut Harvesting

Harvesting mature coconuts directly from the coconut trees in our managed plantations.

Step 02

Washing & Drying

The harvested coconuts undergo a thorough washing process followed by natural drying to prepare them for processing.

Step 03

Crushing

Using specialized crushers to break down the coconut husks into fibers and peat, ensuring optimal texture for further processing.

Step 04

Salinity Washing

Coconuts naturally have high salinity (EC ~2.8). We meticulously wash the material to lower the EC below 0.5 dS/m for safe agricultural use.

Step 05

Natural Drying

We utilize natural sunlight for drying to eliminate pathogens while preserving the beneficial physical properties of the coconut fiber. This is monitored to ensure consistent moisture levels.

Step 06

EC Measurement

Precise Electrical Conductivity (EC) measurement is crucial. We wash and buffer the cocopeat to remove excess salinity, verifying that it meets the specific needs of different crops (Low EC < 0.5dS/m).

Step 07

1st Selection

As fibers and cocopeat are mixed together, we use machines to remove large fibers and other impurities.

Step 08

2nd Selection

After the first selection, the cocopeat undergoes a second screening based on sieve size to further eliminate sand, fibers, and remaining impurities.

Step 09

Final Inspection

The cocopeat that has completed the 2nd selection is checked for specifications once again.

Step 10

Rooting Test

Tests are conducted to ensure that plants can germinate and root in the cocopeat that has finished the final inspection.

Step 11

Block Manufacturing

Cocopeat that has completed rooting and germination tests is manufactured into specific sizes using machinery.

Step 12

Finished Product Check

Water is added to the manufactured block to check the restoration power (expansion), restoration speed, fibers, and impurities.

Step 13

Pre-packaging Product

Before packaging the blocks on pallets, the condition of the blocks is checked and screened.

Step 14

Finished Product Packaging

The blocks that have passed the screening process are stacked and packaged on pallets for ease of loading and unloading. One pallet contains about 220-230 5kg blocks.

Step 15

Container Loading

The packaged pallets are loaded into containers. A 40HQ container holds 20-22 pallets.

Step 16

Shipment & Phytosanitary Certificate Issuance

The container is shipped for ocean transport. The shipping time from India to Korea takes approximately 20 to 25 days.
