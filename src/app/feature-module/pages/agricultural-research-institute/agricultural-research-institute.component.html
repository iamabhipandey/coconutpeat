Our dedicated research institute focuses on improving the quality and efficacy of cocopeat. Through rigorous scientific testing and continuous development, we ensure optimal growth conditions for various crops.

Step 06

EC Measurement

Precise Electrical Conductivity (EC) measurement is crucial. We wash and buffer the cocopeat to remove excess salinity, verifying that it meets the specific needs of different crops (Low EC < 0.5dS/m).

Step 10

Rooting Test

Before mass production, we conduct rooting tests to ensure the medium supports healthy root development. We verify that the physical structure allows for proper aeration and water retention.

Step 05

Natural Drying

We utilize natural sunlight for drying to eliminate pathogens while preserving the beneficial physical properties of the coconut fiber. This is monitored to ensure consistent moisture levels.

Step 07 & 08

Precision Screening

Multi-stage screening processes (1st and 2nd screening) sort the material by particle size. This sorting is critical for creating different grades of cocopeat tailored for specific horticultural uses.
