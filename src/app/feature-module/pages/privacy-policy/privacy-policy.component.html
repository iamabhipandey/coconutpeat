Coconut Peat Privacy Policy

At Coconut Peat (“we”, “us”, “our”), we are committed to protecting your personal information in compliance with applicable laws and regulations. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit or make a purchase from our website (the “Site”).

PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you visit our Site, we automatically gather certain details about your device. This may include your web browser type, IP address, time zone, and information about cookies stored on your device. We also track the specific pages or products you view on our Site, along with the websites or search terms that directed you to us. Collectively, we refer to this data as “Device Information.”

  • Cookies: These are data files placed on your device, containing an anonymous unique identifier.
  • Log files: These track actions on the Site, including IP address, browser type, Internet service provider, referring/exit pages, and date/time stamps.
  • Web beacons, tags, and pixels: These electronic files record information about your browsing activity on the Site.

By using our Site, you consent to third-party services processing your IP address to determine your location for accurate currency conversion. This currency preference is stored in a session cookie within your browser to ensure a consistent shopping experience while you browse our website.

When you make or attempt to make a purchase of Coconut Peat or related products, we collect specific details from you to process your order. This includes your name, billing address, shipping address, payment information, email address, and phone number. We refer to this as “Order Information.”

When we mention “Personal Information” in this Privacy Policy, we are referring to both Device Information and Order Information.

HOW DO WE USE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION?

We use the Order Information collected to process and complete purchases made through the Site. This includes handling payments, arranging shipping, and issuing invoices and order confirmations. Additionally, we may use this information to:

  • Communicate with you regarding your orders;
  • Screen orders for potential risk or fraud; and
  • Provide you with information or advertising related to our eco-friendly products or services, in line with your preferences.

We use the Device Information we collect to help us detect potential risks or fraudulent activities, enhance and optimize the performance of our Site, and evaluate the effectiveness of our marketing and advertising campaigns.

SHARING YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We share your Personal Information with third parties to assist us in using it, as described above. For example, we use trusted third-party logistics companies to deliver our Coir products globally.

CHANGES

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect changes in our practices or for operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.

MINORS

Our Site is not intended for individuals under the age of 18.
