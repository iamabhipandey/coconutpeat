OUR STORY

It started with a simple vision: to transform agricultural waste into wealth for the soil. Harnessing the potential of coconut husks, which were often discarded, we developed a process to turn them into "Black Gold" for plants. Today, our facility processes thousands of tons of raw husk into premium cocopeat blocks, briquettes, and grow bags, serving clients across Europe, Asia, and the Americas.

OUR MISSION

To be the global benchmark in substrate quality and sustainability. We aim to empower farmers to achieve higher yields using less water and fewer chemical inputs. By promoting soil-less farming techniques, we contribute to water conservation and soil health restoration for future generations.

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about using our substrates.

Coco peat (or coir pith) is a 100% natural growing medium made from the husk of coconuts. It is widely used in hydroponics, greenhouse farming, and potting mixes due to its excellent water retention and antifungal properties.

Washed Coco Peat is treated with fresh water to lower its salt content (Low EC), making it ready for sensitive plants. Unwashed/Raw Coco Peat has a natural salt content (High EC) and is typically used for bedding or hardy crops that tolerate salinity.
