Usage: Farms

Excellent adaptability for various agricultural needs

Cocopeat is widely used in modern agriculture due to its superior water retention, aeration, and organic nature. It serves as an essential medium for sustainable farming.

Nursery & Horticulture

Used extensively for flower and vegetable seedlings. It is the preferred substrate for hydroponic cultivation due to its sterile and chemically stable nature.

Organic Fertilizer

Cocopeat is a key ingredient in producing high-quality organic fertilizers. It improves soil texture and aeration, promoting healthy root growth.

Moisture Control

Highly effective for processing food waste and treating livestock manure. Its high absorption capacity helps control moisture and odors efficiently.

Did you know?

Cocopeat acts as a natural soil conditioner. It prevents soil compaction and increases the water-holding capacity of sandy soils while improving drainage in clay soils.

It is 100% biodegradable and eco-friendly, making it the perfect choice for sustainable farming practices.
