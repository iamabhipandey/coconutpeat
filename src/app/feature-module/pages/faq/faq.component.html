What is Coconut Peat?

Coconut peat (cocopeat) is a multi-purpose growing medium made from coconut husk. It is 100% organic, eco-friendly, and meets high standards for agriculture and horticulture.

Why use Cocopeat for plants?

Cocopeat has excellent water retention, high aeration, and natural anti-fungal properties. It helps plant roots grow stronger and faster, making it ideal for hydroponics, potting mixes, and greenhouses.
