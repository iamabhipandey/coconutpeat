What is Cocopeat?

Premium organic growing medium extracted from coconut husks

Cocopeat is an organic substance extracted from coconut fruit. It is clean, pollution-free, and a natural raw material that is widely used as a substitute for soil thanks to its high cellulose content.

National research institutes have verified that Cocopeat has the highest moisture absorption rate among major moisture control materials currently used by domestic farmers.

It is widely used for horticultural soil, potting soil, and organic fertilizer production due to its excellent air permeability, water retention, and affordable price compared to peat moss.

Major sourcing countries: India, Sri Lanka, Indonesia, Vietnam, Philippines.
